## Aesthetic Principles

1. **Dark first** — black and near-black is the canvas, not an afterthought
2. **Gold accent** — sulfur (#D4A852) is the primary accent; green (#00FFAA) is sparing
3. **Geometry over organic** — circles, triangles, hexagons > natural curves
4. **Occult > spooky** — ritual, sigils, alchemy > Halloween horror
5. **Chibi as contrast** — cute mascot against dark conspiracy background
6. **Post-internet** — glitch, scanlines, PCB textures, terminal aesthetics
7. **Maximalism** — layered, dense, conspiracy-board energy > minimalism
